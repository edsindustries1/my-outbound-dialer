Frequently asked questions

What will my total monthly bill actually be?

Your bill has two parts: the flat platform fee ($99 or $399/mo) plus pay-as-you-go usage. Base usage starts at $0.10/dial (carrier fees + AI voicemail synthesis). Optional add-ons are billed per dial on top of the base — Personalized AI Voicemails (+$0.04/dial), Live Transfer (+$0.02/dial), Gatekeeper Navigator (+$0.05/dial), Recording & Transcription (+$0.05/dial), and Voice Cloning (+$19/mo flat). Use the calculator above to see your exact line-by-line estimate based on the features you actually need. No surprise charges — ever.

Can I switch plans later?

Yes. Upgrade or downgrade at any time. Changes take effect on your next billing cycle.

Is there a contract or commitment?

No contracts on monthly plans. Cancel anytime with no penalties. Annual plans are billed upfront for the year and are non-refundable after the 7-day money-back window.

How does annual billing work?

Annual plans are billed as a single upfront payment — $990/year for Starter or $3,990/year for Business. That's equivalent to paying for 10 months and getting 2 months free (≈17% savings). Your account stays active for the full 365 days from the date of payment.

What payment methods do you accept?

We accept all major credit and debit cards through our secure payment processor.

Do you offer refunds?

We offer a 7-day money-back guarantee on all plans. No questions asked.
