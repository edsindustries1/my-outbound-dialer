Frequently asked questions

What will my total monthly bill actually be?

Your bill has two parts: the flat platform fee ($69 Open Humana Starter or $169 Open Humana Sales Floor) plus pay-as-you-go usage. Base dial usage is $0.03/dial (Open Humana Starter) or $0.05/dial (Open Humana Sales Floor). Live transfers are billed per connection event — $0.15 (Open Humana Starter) or $0.20 (Open Humana Sales Floor) — not per dial, so you only pay when a real human picks up and gets bridged to your phone. Optional add-ons: Personalized AI Voicemails (+$0.02/dial), Gatekeeper Navigator (+$0.03/dial), Recording & Transcription (+$0.03/dial), Voice Cloning (+$19/mo), Extra Phone Numbers (+$5/number/mo). Use the calculator above to build your exact line-by-line estimate. No surprise charges — ever.

What is the difference between Open Humana Starter and Open Humana Sales Floor?

Open Humana Starter ($69/mo) is built for solo operators and small teams. Alex dials from a single pool of 3 numbers, drops the same pre-recorded voicemail each time, and transfers live answers to your phone. Open Humana Sales Floor ($169/mo) is a full outbound engine: 10 included numbers, 500 dials per day, and AI-generated voicemails that are uniquely crafted for each contact — their name, their company, their context. If voicemail quality matters to your conversion rate, the Open Humana Sales Floor is the right choice.

How does live transfer billing work?

Live transfers are charged per connection event — meaning you are only billed when a real human answers and Alex successfully bridges the call to your phone. You are never charged for dials that go to voicemail, busy signals, or hang-ups. The calculator estimates transfers at a 20% connect rate, but you only pay for what actually connects.

Can I switch plans later?

Yes. Upgrade or downgrade at any time. Changes take effect on your next billing cycle. Usage from the current cycle is billed at your original plan's rates.

How does annual billing work?

Annual plans are billed as a single upfront payment — $690/year for Open Humana Starter or $1,690/year for Open Humana Sales Floor. That works out to $57.50/mo or $140.83/mo respectively — two months free compared to monthly billing. Your account stays active for the full 365 days from the date of payment.

Is there a contract or refund policy?

No contracts on monthly plans — cancel anytime with no penalties. Annual plans are billed upfront for the year. We offer a 7-day money-back guarantee on all plans, no questions asked.
