## Description (4,000 chars max — paste this into the "Description" field)

```
Open Humana is your AI-powered digital sales employee — an outbound dialer
that works 24/7, never calls in sick, and costs less than your team's monthly
coffee budget.

WHAT ALEX DOES FOR YOUR TEAM
Your reps spend 64% of their time dialing, waiting, and leaving voicemails.
Alex eliminates all of it. He dials your contact list automatically, detects
answering machines with 97% accuracy, drops a personalized voicemail on every
missed call, and instantly transfers live answers directly to your team's
phone.

Your closers only talk to people who actually picked up.

KEY FEATURES
• Automated Campaign Dialer — Upload a CSV and launch a campaign that dials
  hundreds of contacts automatically. No setup calls required.

• Personalized Voicemails — Every voicemail is personalized with the
  prospect's name, company, and custom details using AI voice technology.
  Your callbacks increase up to 5x.

• Instant Live Transfer — The moment a human picks up, the call transfers
  to your team's existing phone in under 2 seconds. They answer it like any
  other inbound call.

• Real-Time Dashboard — Watch every call happen live. See voicemails dropped,
  transfers fired, durations, and outcomes in a real-time activity feed.

• Desktop Notifications — Get an instant macOS notification the moment a
  live transfer fires. Click to open the dashboard and manage the call.

• Menu Bar Status — A quick-glance menu bar indicator shows your campaign
  status (Active/Paused/Idle) at all times without opening the app.

• CRM Integrations — Sync call outcomes directly to HubSpot, GoHighLevel,
  Pipedrive, Google Sheets, and more via webhooks.

• Real-Time Transcription — Every call is transcribed live for instant
  review and reporting.

PRICING
Open Humana Starter — $69/month: 150 dials/day, 3 phone numbers, voicemail
drop, live transfer.

Open Humana Sales Floor — $169/month: 500 dials/day, 10 phone numbers, AI
personalized voicemails, CRM sync.

Custom plans available at openhumana.com/pricing.

This app is free to download. An active Open Humana subscription is required
to use the service (subscribe at openhumana.com).

BUILT FOR
Real estate brokers, insurance agents, SaaS SDR teams, recruiting agencies,
and any sales organization that dials more than 50 contacts per day.
```

## App Review Notes (paste into "Notes for Review" in App Store Connect)
```
This app loads the Open Humana dashboard at https://app.openhumana.com and
adds the following native macOS features that go beyond what a browser provides:

1. DESKTOP NOTIFICATIONS — The app polls /api/native/activity every 15 seconds
   and fires a UNUserNotificationCenter notification when a live call transfer
   occurs. Clicking the notification opens the Live Calls view directly.

2. MENU BAR STATUS ITEM — A persistent NSStatusItem in the macOS menu bar shows
   campaign status (Active/Paused/Idle) with color-coded indicators and quick
   actions (Pause/Resume, Open Dashboard, Quit).

3. NATIVE CSV FILE PICKER — The campaign wizard's file input is intercepted and
   replaced with a native NSOpenPanel dialog filtered to CSV files. The selected
   file is injected back into the web form via DataTransfer API.

DEMO ACCOUNT FOR REVIEW:
Email:    demo@openhumana.com
Password: Demo1234

The demo account has a pre-loaded campaign with sample contacts so you can see
the dashboard, live call feed, and voicemail management features. You can also
test the menu bar status indicator — it will show "Idle" for the demo account.
The desktop notification system requires an active campaign with live transfers
to fire; the demo account's campaign can be launched to trigger notifications.
```
